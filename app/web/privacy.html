Privacy notice

How we handle your order data

Last updated: 28 September 2026 · Draft for legal review (Costa Rica Ley 8968 and EU GDPR for EU visitors).

What we collect

Your name, phone or WhatsApp number, delivery address or beach spot, and any kitchen notes. We also record what you ordered and what it cost.

Why

Only to prepare, deliver and confirm your order (performance of a contract, GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use analytics cookies.

How long we keep it

Contact details are deleted automatically 30 days after your order. The order itself (items, totals, taxes) is kept for accounting and tax purposes, linked only to a one-way code, not to your name or number.

Where it is stored

Encrypted at rest in Amazon Web Services (United States). A backup copy is kept in Microsoft Azure for disaster recovery.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at hola@frostyirie.cr or on WhatsApp +506 8918-5528. We answer within 30 days.
